FORK IN THE WORLD

What happens to your comment when you vote

Hi! Fork in the World is a small one-person channel: a pixel colony on a floating island, and every day you decide what the settlers do next by commenting 1, 2, 3 or 4. Since the whole thing runs on your comments, it's fair that you know exactly what I do with them. Spoiler: almost nothing.

How the counting works

Every day after voting closes (13:00 UTC), I run a small script on my own computer. It asks YouTube for the public comments under that day's Short and looks for a number from 1 to 4 at the start of each one.

To do that it sees what anyone scrolling the comments can see: the text, who posted it (as a channel ID, so one account = one vote), and when it was posted or edited (so late votes don't sneak in). That's it. It can't see your email, your account, your subscriptions or anything private, and you never log in to anything.

What I keep

Only the totals. Something like 1: 12, 2: 4, 3: 9, 4: 2 and how many people voted. The channel IDs and comment texts are thrown away the moment counting ends. They never get saved to a file, a log or a database. I honestly couldn't tell you who voted for what yesterday, and that's on purpose.

The totals end up in the next episode, in the pinned comment and in the season's game history, which I'll publish at the end of the season so anyone can check the votes and the random rolls weren't rigged.

No ads tracking, no selling data, no sharing it with anyone, no cookies on this page. There's simply nothing to sell.

Settler names

At some point I'll ask you to name new settlers. If I pick your suggestion, the name shows up as a character in the story. I won't mention who suggested it unless you want me to, and I read every suggestion myself before using it.

Changed your mind?

Your comment lives on YouTube, so you can edit or delete it whenever you like. Delete it before 13:00 UTC and it won't count. Don't want to vote at all? Just don't start your comment with a number 1–4 and the script will skip it.

The YouTube part

The script uses the YouTube API Services to read comments, so YouTube's own rules apply too: the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The channel isn't aimed at kids, and I don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, kids under 13 included.

Questions

Just leave a comment under any video on the channel (without a number at the start, so it doesn't count as a vote). I read them all anyway, that's kind of the whole point. If anything here ever changes, I'll update this page and the date below.
